# Arm and trunk/abdomen research triage

Status: **candidate wording for human source review; not part of the public edition**

Requested scope: research specific to arm lymphoedema and to swelling described colloquially as “stomach lymphoedema.” For this review, the latter is limited to external truncal, chest-wall, or abdominal-wall lymphoedema. Ascites, internal-organ disease, and abdominal lymphatic malformations are out of scope.

## Proposed inclusions

### Arm and upper limb

- **PMID 41703640 — Aldrich et al. (2026): include as a candidate.** A prospective longitudinal cohort followed arm lymphatic pumping in 51 people receiving treatment for locally advanced breast cancer. It adds a location-specific early-detection and mechanism question that is not covered by the current exercise summary.
- Source access: PubMed record and open-access full text were available on 2026-08-01.
- Human check still required: confirm the drafted sequence of pumping impairment, dermal backflow, and clinical diagnosis against the figures and statistical analysis; assess intervention-related bias, attrition, funding, and conflicts.

### Trunk, chest, and abdominal wall

- **PMID 42132833 — Mazarei et al. (2026): include as a candidate.** A retrospective review mapped 173 hemitrunks in 95 people after breast-cancer treatment. It is recent and directly concerns truncal lymphoedema, an underrepresented body area.
- **PMID 29707465 — Ewalds et al. (2018): include only as supporting context.** This four-patient abdominal-wall case series is directly relevant to the requested abdominal area, but it is very small and reports mainly subjective outcomes.
- Source access: both PubMed records were available; the 2018 open-access full text was checked on 2026-08-01. Only the abstract and citation metadata were available for the 2026 truncal paper during drafting.
- Human check still required: obtain and read the 2026 full text; verify truncal boundaries, staging, selection of the two surgical cases, outcome measurement, follow-up, adverse events, funding, and conflicts.

## Considered but not proposed as separate cards

- **PMID 40081785 — supervised multimodal exercise and surveillance RCT:** directly measures arm outcomes, but substantially overlaps the current edition’s resistance-training prevention summary. A human editor may decide to add it as a supporting source to that item instead.
- **PMID 41886031 — long-term LYMPHA single-centre study:** potentially relevant to arm-lymphoedema prevention, but the non-randomised design and selection over a long treatment era need detailed appraisal before it could alter the brief.
- **PMID 42294341 — network meta-analysis of non-pharmacological breast-cancer-related lymphoedema treatments:** broad arm-treatment evidence, but treatment rankings need a full risk-of-bias and network-connectivity assessment before patient-facing interpretation.
- **PMID 38143840 and PMID 28456757 — massive localised abdominal lymphoedema case reports:** excluded from the general abdominal card because they concern rare, obesity-associated masses and individual surgical cases that may be confused with more typical truncal swelling.
