## Source-review checks

- [x] The established-care summary is linked to Healthdirect, NHS, and International Society of Lymphology guidance.
- [x] The selected-options summary does not imply that newer compression delivery, resistance exercise, or microsurgery is suitable for everyone.
- [x] The evidence-strength summary distinguishes published evidence from registered trials with no posted results.
- [x] The medicines section states that there is no established routine medicine for uncomplicated peripheral lymphoedema itself.
- [x] Antibiotic and diuretic statements are contextualised without giving individual medication instructions.
- [x] The GLP-1 registry entry is presented only as a study with no posted results, not as evidence of benefit or a recommendation.

- [x] “Milroy disease” is explained for readers without medical training.
