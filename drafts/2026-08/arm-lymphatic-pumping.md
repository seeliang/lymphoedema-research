takeaway: "In this high-risk cohort, reduced lymphatic pumping in the treated-side arm appeared before dermal backflow and a clinical breast-cancer-related lymphoedema diagnosis."
meaning: "The sequence may help researchers study earlier detection and prevention, but it does not yet establish a routine screening test or treatment."
limitation: "The study was small and limited to people with locally advanced breast cancer receiving intensive treatment. Attrition, missed visits, and care sought after participants viewed their imaging may have affected the timing of diagnosis, so the results may not apply to people receiving less extensive treatment."
