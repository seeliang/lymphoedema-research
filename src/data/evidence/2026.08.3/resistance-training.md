studyDesign: "Two 2026 systematic reviews and meta-analyses of randomised trials: a prevention-focused review of 11 trials involving 1,450 women and a broader review of 38 trials involving 4,843 participants, interpreted alongside long-term randomised-trial follow-up"

takeaway: "The prevention-focused review found a lower incidence of breast-cancer-related lymphoedema with progressive resistance exercise and rated the evidence as moderately certain. The broader review also reported lower incidence and improved limb outcomes."
meaning: "Appropriately supervised resistance exercise may be considered as part of rehabilitation after breast-cancer treatment, but it does not guarantee prevention or replace individual assessment."
limitation: "Exercise trials cannot blind participants, and training programmes and lymphoedema definitions varied. The broader review reported high heterogeneity and mixed prevention with established-lymphoedema populations; nearly half of the original participants were missing from the long-term follow-up study."
